# Billy Proscia Renovations — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Source:** Muse brief, 2026-10-21; Facebook page metadata (facebook.com/BillyBATS333, fetched 2026-10-21)

## Users
- Homeowners who need a bathroom redone, who find the link on Facebook (group posts, the business's own page) and open it on a phone inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner, who receives the finished site for free and should recognise his own business in it at a glance.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook scroller into a phone call or a text about their bathroom. Quotes are by phone today; the site adds a short form that writes the text for them.
